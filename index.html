The basics

What is nano-silver fluoride?

Three ingredients working together

NSF combines silver nanoparticles, which are antibacterial, with fluoride, which helps remineralize tooth structure, held together by chitosan, a natural polymer.

Developed as an alternative to SDF

Silver diamine fluoride (SDF) is effective at stopping cavities but stains decayed areas black. NSF was formulated to give similar protection with a much lower risk of dark staining.

Painless and quick to apply

A dental professional cleans and dries the tooth, brushes on the liquid, and lets it air dry. There is no drilling and no rinsing.

Evidence library

Current research, summarized

Each summary links to the original paper. Most published trials tested experimental NSF formulas for stopping cavities in children's baby teeth, so results may not apply directly to every product or patient.

Clinical trialBMC Oral Health · 2024

NSF vs SDF for early childhood cavities

Quritum et al. treated 360 children under age 4 in rural Egypt with either one NSF application or two SDF applications, and checked results at 6 and 12 months.

Cavities stopped at 12 months: 71.3% with NSF vs 56.3% with SDF. Parents happy with appearance: 97.2% vs 76.1%.

Systematic reviewEvidence-Based Dentistry · 2024

NSF for arresting cavities in children, one-year follow-up

Shetty et al. screened over 2,500 papers and kept three randomized trials in school children with a full year of follow-up.

NSF beat placebo in two trials, and showed no significant difference from SDF in the trial comparing the two.

Read the study ↗
Meta-analysisJ Indian Soc Pedod Prev Dent · 2022

NSF as a caries-arresting agent in children

Choubey et al. pooled five randomized trials in baby teeth and first permanent molars with at least six months of follow-up.

NSF performed about the same as active treatments like SDF, and clearly better than placebo. The authors call it promising for baby teeth.

Read the study ↗
Clinical trialJournal of Stomatology · 2020

Effectiveness and child acceptance of NSF vs SDF

Al-Nerabieah et al. treated 244 active lesions in 119 preschool children and measured both results and how comfortable the children were.

Arrest at 6 months: 67.2% NSF vs 79.5% SDF, a difference that was not statistically significant. Children accepted both equally.

Read the study ↗
Clinical trialJ Clin Exp Dent · 2019

Nano-silver fluoride varnish vs 38% SDF

Tirupathi et al. ran a double-blind trial in primary molars, comparing a 5% nano-silver sodium fluoride varnish with SDF over 12 months.

No significant difference in cavities stopped at any visit. The nano-silver varnish did not cause dark staining.

Read the study ↗
Clinical trialJournal of Dentistry · 2014

The original "silver bullet" trial

dos Santos et al. applied NSF once to 130 decayed baby teeth and compared it with water, with blinded examiners at 7 days, 5 months and 12 months.

Arrested at 12 months: 66.7% with NSF vs 34.7% with water. Teeth were not stained.

Read the study ↗
Systematic reviewBMC Oral Health · 2020

A more cautious view of silver formulations

Fakhruddin et al. reviewed lab and clinical studies of silver-based products against the bacteria found in cavities.

Lab evidence for nanosilver is strong, but the authors found clinical evidence still thin and asked for more work on particle size, dose and safety.

Read the study ↗
Registered trialClinicalTrials.gov · NCT07861321

ClearDefense vs AdvantageArrest in children

A triple-masked randomized trial registered to compare one application of ClearDefense with one application of 38% SDF on active cavities, with about 72 participants planned.

Results not yet published. This is one of the first trials to test ClearDefense by name.
