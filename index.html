How it works

Three ingredients, one quick step.

Nano-silver fluoride was developed as a gentler alternative to silver diamine fluoride. It pairs the antibacterial power of silver with the protective effect of fluoride, without the dark staining SDF is known for.

Silver · Ag

Fights cavity-causing bacteria

Silver nanoparticles are strongly antibacterial against Streptococcus mutans, the main bacteria behind tooth decay.

Fluoride · F

Strengthens tooth structure

Fluoride helps remineralize enamel and dentin, making the surface harder and more resistant to acid attack.

Chitosan

Holds it all together

A natural, plant-derived polymer that stabilizes the silver particles and helps the solution stay on the tooth.

Sensitivity, up close

Sealing the tiny channels that cause sensitivity

Dentin is full of microscopic tubules that lead to the nerve. When they are open, cold or sweet triggers reach the nerve and cause sharp pain. ClearDefense is designed to occlude these tubules.

2024
Clinical trialBMC Oral Health

NSF vs SDF for early childhood cavities

360 children under age 4 in rural Egypt received either one NSF application or two SDF applications, checked at 6 and 12 months.

Arrested at 12 months: 71.3% NSF vs 56.3% SDF. Parents happy with appearance: 97.2% vs 76.1%.

2024
Systematic reviewEvidence-Based Dentistry

NSF in children, one-year follow-up

Shetty et al. screened over 2,500 papers and kept three randomized trials in school children with a full year of follow-up.

NSF beat placebo in two trials and showed no significant difference from SDF in the third.

2022
Meta-analysisJ Indian Soc Pedod Prev Dent

NSF as a caries-arresting agent in children

Choubey et al. pooled five randomized trials in baby teeth and first permanent molars with at least six months of follow-up.

NSF performed about the same as active treatments like SDF and clearly better than placebo.

2020
Clinical trialJournal of Stomatology

Effectiveness and child acceptance, NSF vs SDF

244 active lesions in 119 preschool children, measuring both results and how comfortable the children were.

Arrested at 6 months: 67.2% NSF vs 79.5% SDF, not statistically significant. Children accepted both equally.

Read the study ↗
2020
Systematic reviewBMC Oral Health

A more cautious view of silver formulations

Fakhruddin et al. reviewed lab and clinical studies of silver-based products against the bacteria found in cavities.

Lab evidence for nanosilver is strong, but clinical evidence was still thin. The authors called for more work on particle size, dose and safety.

2019
Clinical trialJ Clin Exp Dent

Nano-silver fluoride varnish vs 38% SDF

A double-blind trial in primary molars comparing a 5% nano-silver sodium fluoride varnish with SDF over 12 months.

No significant difference in lesions arrested at any visit, and the nano-silver varnish caused no dark staining.

2014
Clinical trialJournal of Dentistry

The original "silver bullet" trial

One NSF application on 130 decayed baby teeth, compared with water, with blinded examiners at 7 days, 5 months and 12 months.

Arrested at 12 months: 66.7% NSF vs 34.7% water. Teeth were not stained.

Questions

Frequently asked

Have a question about your own teeth? Your dentist is the best person to ask.

What is nano-silver fluoride?

A liquid that combines tiny silver particles, fluoride and chitosan. The silver fights bacteria, the fluoride strengthens teeth, and the chitosan holds the mixture together. It is brushed directly onto the tooth by a dental professional.

Does it stain teeth black like SDF?

Studies report far less staining than silver diamine fluoride. The maker of ClearDefense says its low silver content significantly reduces the risk, though some discoloration can still happen depending on how damaged the dentin is.

Is it safe?

The clinical trials summarized here reported it was well tolerated, including in young children. Some reviewers have asked for more research into particle size, dose and long-term safety. People with a silver allergy should not use it. Your dentist can tell you whether it suits you.

Is ClearDefense used to treat cavities?

ClearDefense is indicated for treating tooth sensitivity in adults. Most cavity research used other nano-silver fluoride formulas. A trial testing ClearDefense on cavities in children is registered, and we will add the results when they are published.

Can I buy it for home use?

No. ClearDefense is sold only to licensed dental professionals and is applied in the dental office. Ask your dentist if they offer it.

How is it applied?

The dentist cleans the tooth with a fluoride-free paste, isolates and dries the area, brushes on the solution, and lets it air dry. There is no drilling, no rinsing, and no eating or drinking restrictions afterward.
