How it works

Three ingredients, one quick step

Nano-silver fluoride was developed as a gentler alternative to silver diamine fluoride. It combines the antibacterial power of silver with the protective effect of fluoride, without the dark staining SDF is known for.

Ag47

Fights cavity-causing bacteria

Silver nanoparticles are strongly antibacterial against Streptococcus mutans, the main bacteria behind tooth decay.

F9

Strengthens tooth structure

Fluoride helps remineralize enamel and dentin, making the surface harder and more resistant to acid attack.

Chpolymer

Holds it all together

Chitosan, a natural polymer, stabilizes the silver particles and helps the solution stay on the tooth. ClearDefense uses a plant-derived source.

Sensitivity, up close

Sealing the tiny channels that cause sensitivity

Dentin is full of microscopic tubules that lead to the nerve. When they are open, cold or sweet triggers reach the nerve and cause sharp pain. ClearDefense is designed to occlude these tubules.

Green-synthesized NSF rebuilds enamel without staining

Palankalieva et al. compared NSF with SDF and SDF plus potassium iodide on artificial enamel lesions in baby teeth.

Two NSF applications restored hardness about as well as SDF/KI. SDF stained black by day 1; NSF showed no color change at any point.

Clinical trialBMC Oral Health

NSF vs SDF for early childhood cavities

Quritum et al. treated 360 children under age 4 in rural Egypt with one NSF application or two SDF applications.

Arrested at 12 months: 71.3% NSF vs 56.3% SDF. Parents happy with appearance: 97.2% vs 76.1%.

Systematic reviewEvidence-Based Dentistry

NSF in children, one-year follow-up

Shetty et al. screened over 2,500 papers and kept three randomized trials in school children with a full year of follow-up.

NSF beat placebo in two trials and showed no significant difference from SDF in the third.

Thimmaiah et al. reviewed about 28 studies on NSF across caries management, orthodontics and restorative dentistry.

Concluded NSF may be an effective, affordable and improved anticaries agent, especially for minimally invasive care.

Clinical trialBrazilian Dental Science

NSF vs SDF in deep cavities in permanent molars

Ahmed Aly et al. treated 36 patients with deep cavities in lower molars, using NSF, SDF or neither before a filling.

Success at 6 months: 100% NSF, 91.7% SDF, 83.3% control. Differences were not statistically significant.

NSF as a caries-arresting agent in children

Choubey et al. pooled five randomized trials in baby teeth and first permanent molars with at least six months of follow-up.

NSF performed about the same as active treatments like SDF and clearly better than placebo.

Clinical trialJournal of Stomatology

Effectiveness and child acceptance, NSF vs SDF

Al-Nerabieah et al. treated 244 active lesions in 119 preschool children and measured results and comfort.

Arrested at 6 months: 67.2% NSF vs 79.5% SDF, not statistically significant. Children accepted both equally.

A more cautious view of silver formulations

Fakhruddin et al. reviewed lab and clinical studies of silver products against the bacteria found in cavities.

Lab evidence for nanosilver is strong, but clinical evidence was still thin. Called for more work on particle size, dose and safety.

Safety & open questions

What we know, and what's still being studied

Nano-silver fluoride is promising, and the research is still young. Here is a straightforward look at both sides.

What the evidence supports

  • Stops cavities in baby teeth. Reviews pooling several randomized trials found NSF works about as well as SDF and clearly better than placebo.

  • Little or no black staining. Several trials from 2014 to 2024 and a 2025 lab study reported no dark staining with NSF.

  • Well accepted by children. Pain and comfort scores were similar to SDF, and parents preferred how NSF-treated teeth looked.

  • Helps rebuild enamel in the lab. Two applications of NSF restored enamel hardness about as well as SDF with potassium iodide.

Still being studied

  • ?

    Formulas vary. Studies used different silver concentrations and particle sizes, so results from one product do not automatically apply to another.

  • ?

    Mostly tested in children. Few published trials include adults, root surfaces or long follow-up beyond 12 months.

  • ?

    Long-term silver exposure. Reviewers recommend more research on particle size, dose and whether silver builds up over time.

  • !

    Allergies. People allergic to silver should not use it. Some NSF formulas use shellfish-derived chitosan; ClearDefense's chitosan is plant-derived.

Questions

Frequently asked

Have a question about your own teeth? Your dentist is the best person to ask.

What is nano-silver fluoride?+

A liquid that combines tiny silver particles, fluoride and chitosan. The silver fights bacteria, the fluoride strengthens teeth, and the chitosan holds the mixture together. A dental professional brushes it directly onto the tooth.

Who is Dr. David Keeton?+

Dr. Keeton is a dentist in San Antonio, Texas, who focuses on early, minimally invasive care. He partners with Young Specialties on ClearDefense Silver Fluoride. You can hear him explain his approach in the video at the top of this page.

Does it stain teeth black like SDF?+

Studies report far less staining than silver diamine fluoride, and a 2025 lab study found no color change at all. The maker of ClearDefense says its low silver content significantly reduces the risk, though some discoloration can still happen depending on how damaged the dentin is.

Is it safe?+

The clinical trials summarized here reported it was well tolerated, including in young children. Reviewers have asked for more research into particle size, dose and long-term silver exposure. People with a silver allergy should not use it. Your dentist can tell you whether it suits you.

Is ClearDefense used to treat cavities?+

ClearDefense is indicated for treating tooth sensitivity in adults. Most cavity research used other nano-silver fluoride formulas. A trial testing ClearDefense on cavities in children is registered, and this site will add the results when they are published.

Can I buy it for home use?+

No. ClearDefense is sold only to licensed dental professionals and is applied in the dental office. Ask your dentist if they offer it.

How is it applied?+

The dentist cleans the tooth with a fluoride-free paste, isolates and dries the area, brushes on the solution, and lets it air dry. There is no drilling, no rinsing, and no eating or drinking restrictions afterward.
